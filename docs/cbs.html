Parameter settings

The knobs behind every number on this page, in the two groups src/provenance.py partitions them into, which is also why they are read from different places (a third column regroups the cadence-coupled ones, which are pulled out of these two rather than repeated). A curator knob acts upstream of the journal, so the honest value is the one stamped in data/cbs_v14/provenance.json when the curation ran: investor_profile.forward.md is a live file, and a knob edited there since would describe a curation that could not have produced this journal. An optimizer knob acts at replay time, and this build just replayed the journal under the profile as it stands now, so that is where those come from. Changing a curator knob means buying the curation again, while changing an optimizer knob costs a rebuild. The caps bound what this page can show: max_events and max_new_events limit how many events a scan may open, and max_watchlist limits how many the optimizer may fund, so read them alongside Breadth over time and Watchlist composition.
